A Stitch in the Dark

Published Sep 23, 2026 · ~454 pages · ASIN B0HKSJDCC4

Dark Fantasy Urban Fantasy New release

For forty years, he was the monster in the shadows. Now he has to become something worse to save the only person he has left.

New York’s elite don’t just have money. They have appetites — and Stitch has spent four decades feeding them.

He was Robert once: a scared kid from the Bowery who watched his brother die at an exclusive party that turned out to be a slaughterhouse. To survive, he made a deal with a monster wearing a bespoke suit and a silver-eyed smile. He traded his humanity for protection, and became something else — a hound who lures the desperate and the forgotten into the jaws of the fallen, one perfect promise of hope at a time.

He tells himself it’s just a job. He tells himself the hollow ache where his soul used to be doesn’t count as a conscience.

Then he meets a boy who reminds him exactly who he used to be — and for the first time in forty years, Stitch does the one thing a hound is never supposed to do.

He runs. With the boy. Straight into the crosshairs of the creature who made him.

What follows is a war fought across continents — from the ashes of New York to a secret Vatican order armed with silver and centuries of hidden history — as Stitch races to outrun the darkest thing he’s ever fought: himself, and the price of finally choosing to be human again.

A Stitch in the Dark is a blood-soaked, high-stakes urban fantasy about corruption, redemption, and what’s left of a man’s soul after he’s sold every other piece of it. For readers of Richard Kadrey’s Sandman Slim — dark, violent, unflinching urban fantasy where hell has a hierarchy and the monsters wear tailored suits.
