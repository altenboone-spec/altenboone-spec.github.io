The writer

The worlds in his head were never for sale.

Two years in the Royal Air Force. Twenty-six as a salesman. And every spare hour spent chasing the stories that got under his skin as a kid — the ones where ordinary towns feel haunted, and home is science fiction, fantasy, and horror.

Alten Boone is the author of The Scrapyard Chronicles, the horror anthology Antiseptic Illusion, and the novella Jacob’s Rest. He’s writing toward the day this becomes the day job — one book at a time.
