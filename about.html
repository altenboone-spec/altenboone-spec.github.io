About Alten Boone

Science fiction, fantasy, and horror — written in the hours the day job leaves him.

Alten Boone spent two years in the Royal Air Force and the twenty-six years after that as a salesman — but the worlds in his head were never for sale. He was introduced to the work of Stephen King as a kid by his cousin, and something about the way King made ordinary towns feel haunted never let go of him. Science fiction, fantasy, and horror have been home ever since.

He is the author of The Scrapyard Chronicles, beginning with The Scrapyard and continuing in Terminus, along with the horror anthology Antiseptic Illusion and the novella Jacob's Rest. His stories tend to start with ordinary people — commuters, miners, gang kids just trying to survive the night — and put them somewhere they were never meant to go.

Alten writes in whatever hours the day job leaves him, chasing the stories that have been stuck inside him since he was a boy. He's working toward the day he gets to do this full-time, one book at a time.
