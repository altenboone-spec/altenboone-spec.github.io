The Scrapyard Chronicles · Book 2

Terminus

Survival science fiction · Published Mar 6, 2026 · Kindle · Paperback · Hardcover · ISBN 9798251000399

Survival Sci-Fi Series

The Scrapyard Chronicles continue. Having survived their first nights in a world that collects the wreckage of other realities, the remaining survivors push deeper into hostile territory. Help came at a cost — and the path to anything like safety runs through places where death is constant and mercy is rare.

Terminus picks up the fight where The Scrapyard left off: ordinary people, stranded far from Earth, forced to decide how far they will go to stay alive.
